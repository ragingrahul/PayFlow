# Product vision

PayFlow is a modern AI-native payroll management application for small and distributed companies. **Payroll that thinks before you pay.**

## Target users

HR managers, founders, finance teams, and operations teams.

## Core problems

Traditional payroll requires repetitive forms and tables. PayFlow adds a conversational operational layer while keeping calculations deterministic and requiring human approval.

## Core product areas

Company/workspace management, employees, compensation, bonuses, deductions, reimbursements, contractors, payroll runs/history, employee payroll view, scenarios, and AI Payroll Copilot.

## Hero experience (future)

“Give Engineering a 7% raise next month.” Retrieve employees, create and validate a structured proposal, calculate impact in application code, preview, wait for explicit approval, apply, retrieve and verify state, record activity. AI cannot silently change money.

## Non-goals for hackathon MVP

Real bank transfers; full tax compliance; EPF/ESI; government filing; production accounting integrations; Workday/SAP/Rippling integrations; enterprise RBAC complexity.

## Demo story

Milestone 1: open the INR demo workspace, inspect real employee and payroll metrics, open a person, create September 2026 payroll, calculate approved bonuses/reimbursements/deductions, inspect persisted employee items, observe dashboard and activity updates without refreshing. Future milestones add a raise simulation and a confirmed AI bonus proposal.
