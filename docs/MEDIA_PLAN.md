## Recommended first video

**Concept:** a 24-second, silent-first X clip that opens with the HR question, proves the result inside real Scenario Mode, and ends on the safety statement and tagline.

**Framework:** Hook → Proof → Call to action.  
**Audience:** a technical Modern Stack Hackathon judge scrolling X.  
**Desired response:** recognize PayFlow and open the eventual live demo.  
**Vibe:** confident, precise, and product-led.  
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, 16:9; text must work with audio muted.

### Storyboard

| Time   | Scene    | On-screen content                                                             |
| ------ | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–2s   | Hook     | “What would an 8% Engineering raise cost?” over the live Scenario page.       |
| 2–7s   | Input    | Open New scenario, choose Engineering, enter 8%. Keep cursor movement direct. |
| 7–14s  | Proof    | Hold on 8 people, ₹21.10L → ₹21.81L, +₹71,200/month, +₹8.54L/year.            |
| 14–19s | Safety   | Highlight “Saving stores this preview only” and overlay “₹0 changed.”         |
| 19–24s | End card | PayFlow logo, “Payroll that thinks before you pay,” and `[PUBLIC URL]`.       |

Use one smooth zoom vocabulary and simple fades. Avoid stock footage, fake typing, AI imagery, and an opening logo animation. The first frame must already contain the question.

## Follow-up clips

### AI payroll action — 25 seconds

Record only after a real provider is configured. Show the prompt, structured proposal, deterministic impact, explicit confirmation, and resulting pending adjustment. Do not cut directly from confirmation to changed payroll; show the separate Adjustments approval checkpoint.

### Convex realtime — 18 seconds

Use two synchronized browser views for October. Submit and approve one fictional adjustment. Keep the dashboard, employee projection, and activity update visible without refresh. Reset or use a disposable future period before recording another take.

### Full hackathon demo — 60 seconds

Open with Scenario Mode, then move through Copilot, normal approval, realtime dashboard, processed payslip, and the employee portal. Use the longer `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` as narration guidance, but keep this cut centered on observable product behavior.

## Production gate

No Remotion project or final video has been generated yet. Video production starts after approval of the recommended creative direction and confirmation of:

- the final public URL or the decision to omit it from pre-launch clips;
- whether the first cut should be silent-only or include music/voiceover;
- the submission deadline.

The current recommendation is a silent-first Scenario Mode clip with light interface sound effects and no voiceover.
