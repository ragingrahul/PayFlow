## What to record

- Use the platform’s displayed impressions, likes, replies, and reposts.
- Name sponsor engagement factually, for example “@convex replied with a question.”
- Put qualitative feedback or follow-up ideas in Notes.
- Keep deleted, failed, or corrected posts in the log with an explanation rather than erasing history.
